## ICEST (International Scientific Conference on Information, Communication and Energy Systems and Technologies)

- Hosts rotate among three faculties: Telecommunications at TU Sofia (Bulgaria), Electronic
  Engineering at the University of Nis (Serbia), and Technical Sciences at St. Kliment Ohridski
  University, Bitola (North Macedonia). The brief's "Bulgaria and North Macedonia" is
  incomplete. [https://icestconf.org/history-of-icest-conference/]
- ICEST 2025 was the 60th edition, in Ohrid, North Macedonia (Crossref record of Alex's
  ICEST paper, event name and location). [https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1109/ICEST66328.2025.11098436]
- ICEST 2026 was the 61st, in Nis, Serbia, 1 to 3 July 2026. [https://icestconf.org/]
- Length: "Maximal length of the regular papers is 6 pages, and for invited paper 10 pages."
  English only. [https://icestconf.org/2016/01/10/instructions-for-authors/]
- Format: IEEE conference template, Word (A4) or LaTeX in conference mode, or the IEEE Overleaf
  template. [https://icestconf.org/2016/01/10/instructions-for-authors/]
- Indexing: "ICEST 2026 is indexed in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library under IEEE Conference
  Record #71230" [https://icestconf.org/]; accepted 2026 papers "will be submitted for inclusion
  in IEEE Xplore" [https://icestconf.org/post-conference-publications/]. Scopus and Web of
  Science for the proceedings: not stated on the official page (the Scopus and SCIE line on
  the home page refers to the partner journal Sensors). [https://icestconf.org/]
- Extended versions of selected papers go to Facta Universitatis, Microwave Review and
  Horizons. [https://icestconf.org/post-conference-publications/]
- Fee (2026, foreign participants; includes one paper): before 15 June 2026, 300 EUR full,
  270 EUR IEEE member or student; after, 350 EUR and 320 EUR. [https://icestconf.org/conference-fee/]
- Review: "evaluated by two reviewers and by the anonymous expert member of the Editorial
  board". Single or double blind: not stated on the official page.
  [https://icestconf.org/peer-review-process/]
- 2026 dates, for reference: submission 17 April 2026 (soft) and 30 April 2026 (hard),
  notification 29 May 2026, final paper 8 June 2026, conference 1 to 3 July 2026.
  [https://icestconf.org/important-dates-2025/] (the URL says 2025; the content is 2026)
- 2027 deadlines: not published as of 2026-09-27. [https://icestconf.org/]

## COMSCI (International Scientific Conference COMPUTER SCIENCE, TU Sofia)

- It exists: the 14th International Scientific Conference COMPUTER SCIENCE, 13 to 15 September
  2026, Sozopol, run by the Faculty of Computer Systems and Technologies of TU Sofia with the
  IEEE Bulgaria Section, technically co-sponsored by IEEE (conference record #71283).
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252]
- The label "COMSCI" was not seen on the official page (only in IEEE Xplore titles in search
  results). A different, unrelated virtual event "COMSCI 2026" exists at comsci2026.org.
  [https://comsci2026.org/]
- Length: the official template says papers "should not be shorter than 4 pages"; no maximum
  is stated. [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/files/252/Guide_CS_2026.docx], linked from
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252&r=883]
- Format: the conference's own Word template, A4, two columns; no LaTeX template offered; full
  papers in .doc and .pdf. [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252&r=891]
- Indexing: "All accepted and presented papers will be submitted for possible inclusion into
  IEEE Xplore and Scopus database" [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252]. Web of
  Science: not stated on the official page.
- Fee (2026): 360 EUR per paper, early fee 300 EUR, 15% off for students and IEEE members.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252&r=884]
- Review: "Each paper will be reviewed by two reviewers", with a plagiarism check (IEEE
  CrossCheck / iThenticate). Blind model: not stated on the official page.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252&r=891]
- 2026 dates, for reference: paper submission 1 to 15 June 2026, notification 15 to 20 July
  2026, early fee 1 September 2026. [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=252&r=886]
- 2027 deadlines: not published as of 2026-09-27 (no 2027 entry in the TU Sofia e-conf list).
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/]

## TELECOM (National Conference with International Participation, Sofia)

- TELECOM 2026 is the 34th, 26 to 27 November 2026, Sofia, technically co-sponsored by IEEE.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37] [https://ceec.fnts.bg/telecom/information.html]
- Length: "The papers Type [1] should not exceed 4 pages including all tables, figures and
  references (A4, double column)." [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=86]
- Format: IEEE template as .doc; no LaTeX template listed. Type [1] papers go to IEEE Xplore;
  Type [2] (English) to the journal "Electrotechnica & Electronica"; Type [3] (Bulgarian) on CD.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=86]
- Indexing: "Accepted and presented papers will be submitted to the IEEE Xplore Digital
  Library, as well as for indexing in Scopus" [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=83].
  Web of Science: not stated on the official page.
- Fee (2026): regular 250 EUR before 26 November 2026 and 275 EUR after; student 150 EUR and
  165 EUR; 15% off for IEEE members. Which paper type these cover is not stated in the text read.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=86]
- Review: "Each paper will be reviewed by two reviewers." Blind model: not stated on the
  official page. At most two papers per author. [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=83]
- 2026 dates, for reference: paper submission until 2 November 2026, acceptance notification
  until 18 November 2026, fee payment until 24 November 2026.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=89]
- 2027 deadlines: not published as of 2026-09-27.
  [https://e-university.tu-sofia.bg/e-conf/?konf=37&r=89]

  [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics/instructions]

  Compendex". [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics]

  Electrical and Electronic), CiteScore Q1 (Signal Processing); the stats page adds JCR Q3 in
  Physics, Applied and in Computer Science, Information Systems.
  [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics] [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics/stats]

- APC: CHF 2400; no year stated. [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics/apc]
- Review: single-blind, at least two reports; optional open peer review.
  [https://www.mdpi.com/journal/electronics/instructions]
- Deadlines: a journal, so no conference deadline; no page read mentions a 2027 deadline.

## Cybernetics and Information Technologies (CIT, ISSN 1311-9702, eISSN 1314-4081)

- Publisher: Bulgarian Academy of Sciences (IICT), "In partnership with: Paradigm Publishing
  Services"; sciendo.com/journal/CAIT now redirects to reference-global.com.
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT]
- Length: "20 pages for scientific papers and 32 pages for surveys".
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=submission-guidelines]
- Format: Microsoft Word only, with the CIT template, submitted by email.
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=submission-guidelines]
- Indexing: Scopus and Web of Science Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI).
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=indexing]
- Metrics as the page shows them, year not labelled (charts end at 2025): Impact Factor 1.7,
  5-Year IF 1.4, CiteScore 4.2, SJR 0.456. Quartile: not stated on the official page.
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=metrics]
- APC: 600 EUR; fast track 1200 EUR; no year stated.
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=article-processing-charges]
- Review: "A single-blind review is applied", at least two experts.
  [https://reference-global.com/journal/CAIT?tab=peer-review]
- Deadlines: a journal, so no conference deadline; no page read mentions a 2027 deadline.

## SoftwareX (Elsevier, ISSN 2352-7110)

- Length: "Maximum word count: 4000", excluding title, authors, affiliations, references and
